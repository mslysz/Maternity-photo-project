I look forward to hearing from you

Please get in touch with me to discuss the details of a newborn or maternity photo shoot.

Choosing your maternity and newborn photographer is such an important decision. You'll be sharing some of the most fragile, precious moments of your life with them, trusting them to capture your memories exactly as you want to remember them. You need to find someone who makes you feel safe to be vulnerable, and whose art truly makes your heart sing.

If you feel that person might be me, I would love to hear from you. To ensure the highest level of care and dedication for every story I tell, I only accept a limited number of bookings each month, and my calendar fills up quickly. If you love my vision and want me to capture your maternity or newborn journey, please fill out the contact form below or reach out directly at: patricenebraska@gmail.com.
