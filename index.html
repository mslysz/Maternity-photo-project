MATERNITY AND NEWBORN PHOTOGRAPHER

Your story, soulfully captured...

I'm glad you're here...

My name is Patrice, and I’m a lover of children’s photography.

There is nothing more beautiful than capturing the first days of a newborn’s life and, at the same time, bearing witness to the wonderful adventure that is just beginning.

I’m a mother myself, and I know just how special this moment is for every parent.

It is with great joy and dedication that I capture the most beautiful moments of newborn babies, as well as those of older children.

I offer photo shoots in my own studio or in the comfort of your own home. The choice is yours, and I will do my very best to meet your requirements.
